# Shallow-foundation, seabed and anchor geotechnical engines

Reference for `src/engine/geotech/{bearing,mudmat,settlement,earthpressure,liquefaction,scour,suction,anchors,slope,soilmodels,siteresponse,seepage}.js`.

All quantities are SI (m, kg, s, N, Pa, N/m³, rad) unless a unit is stated; strip footings and walls are per metre run.
Each line gives the export with its signature, what it computes (with its source) and the returned fields with units.
Argument units, validity ranges and the full formulas are in the JSDoc of each function; the benchmarks are in
`tests/engine/geotech.<file>.test.js`. Coefficients that are charts in the source, or that could not be confirmed
against it, are arguments rather than constants; section "Limits and open points" lists them.

## bearing.js — Bearing capacity, sliding, overturning

Sources: Terzaghi K. (1943) Theoretical Soil Mechanics, Wiley. Meyerhof G.G. (1963) Some recent research on the bearing capacity of foundations, Can. Geotech. J. 1(1). Brinch Hansen J. (1970) A revised and extended formula for bearing capacity, DGI Bulletin 28. Vesic A.S. (1973) Analysis of ultimate loads of shallow foundations, JSMFD ASCE 99(SM1); Vesic A.S. (1975) Bearing capacity of shallow foundations, Foundation Engineering Handbook, ch. 3. Skempton A.W. (1951) The bearing capacity of clays, Building Research Congress. Davis E.H. & Booker J.R. (1973) Geotechnique 23(4). Meyerhof G.G. & Hanna A.M. (1978) Can. Geotech. J. 15(4); Brown J.D. & Meyerhof G.G. (1969). API RP 2A-WSD (21st ed.) 6.13 and commentary; API RP 2GEO (2011) 7; ISO 19901-4:2016 7; DNV-RP-C212.

- `bearingNq(phi)` — Prandtl–Reissner surcharge factor Nq = e^(π tan φ) tan²(π/4 + φ/2). Returns `number`: Nq [-].
- `bearingNc(phi)` — Prandtl cohesion factor Nc = (Nq − 1) cot φ, with the φ → 0 limit 2 + π. Returns `number`: Nc [-].
- `bearingNgamma(phi, form = 'vesic')` — Self-weight factor Nγ by the named author. Returns `number`: Nγ [-].
- `terzaghiFactors(phi, opts = {})` — Terzaghi (1943) factors for a rough base (general shear). Returns `{Nc:number,Nq:number,Ngamma:number}`: factors [-].
- `terzaghiBearing({ shape, B, c = 0, phi = 0, gamma, q = 0, Kpgamma })` — Terzaghi (1943) ultimate bearing pressure for vertical central load. Returns `{qult:number,Nc:number,Nq:number,Ngamma:number}`: qult gross ultimate pressure [Pa].
- `skemptonNc({ B, L = Infinity, D = 0, form = 'chart' })` — Skempton (1951) Nc for undrained clay with embedment and shape. Skempton's chart runs from 5.14 (strip) and 6.2 (square/circle) at the surface to 7.5 and 9.0 at depth, with Nc(rectangle) = (0.84 + 0.16 B/L) Nc(square). Two documented fits are offered. Returns `number`: Nc [-].
- `effectiveAreaRect({ B, L, eB = 0, eL = 0 })` — Meyerhof effective area of an eccentrically loaded rectangular footing: B' = B − 2 eB, L' = L − 2 eL. The returned B' is always the shorter effective side. Returns `{Beff:number,Leff:number,Aeff:number,swapped:boolean}`: [m, m, m^2 (m^2/m for a strip)]; swapped is true when the effective short side lies along the original L.
- `effectiveAreaCircle({ R, e })` — Effective area of an eccentrically loaded circular footing and its equivalent rectangle (API RP 2A-WSD C6.13 / API RP 2GEO 7.3; DNV-RP-C212 / DNV-ST-0126 App. G). Returns `{Aeff:number,Beff:number,Leff:number,be:number,le:number}`: [m^2, m, m, m, m]; be and le are the minor and major axes of the lens-shaped effective area.
- `bearingCapacity(o)` — General bearing-capacity equation. Returns `{qult:number,Qult:number,Aeff:number,Beff:number,Leff:number,Nc:number,Nq:number,Ngamma:number, factors:object,terms:{cohesion:number,surcharge:number,selfWeight:number}}`: qult gross ultimate pressure on the effective area [Pa]; Qult = qult·A' [N] ([N/m] for a strip).
- `davisBookerStrip({ su0, kappa, B, F = 1 })` — Undrained bearing capacity for strength increasing linearly with depth, su = su0 + κ z (Davis & Booker 1973): qult = F [(2 + π) su0 + κ B / 4] for a strip under vertical load. The correction factor F is a function of κB/su0 and of base roughness that Davis & Booker gave as a chart (F = 1 at κ = 0; roughly 1.0–1.5 for a rough base). It is not reproduced here: supply the chart value. Returns `{qult:number,kappaBoverSu0:number}`: qult net ultimate pressure [Pa]; κB/su0 [-].
- `isoScv(kappaBoverSu0)` — Shape-factor coefficient scv for vertical loading on clay with strength increasing with depth, as tabulated in ISO 19901-4:2016 / API RP 2GEO (after Salençon & Matar 1982). Returns `number`: scv [-].
- `isoUndrainedCapacity(o)` — Undrained capacity of a shallow foundation to ISO 19901-4:2016 7.2 / API RP 2GEO 7.3.1. Returns `{Qd:number,qd:number,Kc:number,sc:number,dc:number,ic:number,bc:number,gc:number,Aeff:number}`: Qd design capacity [N]; qd [Pa].
- `apiDrainedCapacity(o)` — Drained (or general c–φ) capacity with the correction factors of API RP 2A-WSD 6.13.2 and commentary. Returns `{Q:number,q:number,Kc:number,Kq:number,Kgamma:number,Nc:number,Nq:number,Ngamma:number,Aeff:number}`: Q ultimate vertical capacity [N]; q [Pa].
- `punchThroughMeyerhofHanna({ B, L = Infinity, H, D = 0, gamma1, phi1, Ks, ca = 0, qb, qt = Infinity })` — Punch-through of a footing on a strong layer over weak clay or sand (Meyerhof & Hanna 1978). Returns `{qult:number,qPunch:number,governs:'punch-through'|'upper-layer'}`: [Pa].
- `punchThroughLoadSpread({ B, H, ns, su, NcScDc = 1.2 * (2 + PI), p0 = 0, sandWeight = 0 })` — Load-spread (projected-area) punch-through check for a circular footing or spudcan on sand over clay (ISO 19905-1 A.9.3.2.6.4 / SNAME T&R 5-5A form): the load spreads at 1 horizontal : ns vertical through the sand and bears on the clay over an enlarged circle. Returns `{Qv:number,Bequiv:number}`: Qv ultimate vertical load [N]; equivalent diameter at clay level [m].
- `squeezingCapacity({ B, T, D = 0, su, p0 = 0, a = 5, b = 0.33, qGeneral })` — Squeezing of a soft clay layer of thickness T between the footing and a strong stratum (Meyerhof & Chaplin 1953 as used in SNAME T&R 5-5A / ISO 19905-1 A.9.3.2.6.5). Returns `{qult:number,squeezing:boolean,qSqueeze:number,qGeneral:number}`: [Pa].
- `punchThroughClayOverClay({ B, H, D = 0, suTop, suBottom, p0 = 0, kt = 3, NcSc = 6 })` — Punch-through of strong clay over weak clay for a circular footing (Brown & Meyerhof 1969 as adopted in SNAME T&R 5-5A / ISO 19905-1). Returns `{qult:number,qPunch:number,qTop:number,governs:string}`: [Pa].
- `slidingResistance(o)` — Sliding resistance of a shallow foundation with optional skirts. Returns `{H:number,Hbase:number,Hpassive:number,Hactive:number}`: resistances [N].
- `overturningCheck({ V, M, B, shape = 'rect' })` — Overturning and uplift check of a rigid footing from statics. Returns `{e:number,fsOverturning:number,kernLimit:number,fullContact:boolean,contactWidth:number}`: e [m]; fsOverturning [-]; kernLimit [m]; contactWidth = B − 2e [m].
- `designCheck({ load, resistance, safetyFactor = 1, loadFactor = 1, materialFactor = 1 })` — Design verification in either working-stress or partial-factor (LRFD) format. Returns `{uc:number,designLoad:number,designResistance:number,ok:boolean}`: uc = demand/capacity [-].

## mudmat.js — Mudmats

Sources: API RP 2GEO (2011) 7 and Annex A; ISO 19901-4:2016 7; DNV-RP-C212 (2019) 5–6. Green A.P. (1954) The plastic yielding of metal junctions due to combined shear and pressure, J. Mech. Phys. Solids 2 (exact V–H interaction of a strip on Tresca material). Meyerhof G.G. (1953) The bearing capacity of foundations under eccentric and inclined loads. Bransby M.F. & Randolph M.F. (1998) Geotechnique 48(5); Gourvenec S. (2007) Geotechnique 57(9); Feng X., Randolph M.F., Gourvenec S. & Wallerand R. (2014) Geotechnique 64(1). DNV-RP-H103 / DNV-RP-N103 6 (landing on the seabed, retrieval).

- `greenVH(h)` — Exact V–H interaction of a rough strip on uniform Tresca soil (Green 1954). Returns `number`: largest admissible V/(A su) [-] (2 + π at h = 0, 1 + π/2 at h = 1).
- `greenHofV(v)` — Inverse of greenVH: limiting H/(A su) for a given vertical mobilisation v = V/Vult. Returns `number`: h = H/(A su) [-].
- `torsionalCapacityRect({ B, L, su })` — Plastic torsional capacity of a rectangular base in full contact on undrained soil. Returns `number`: Tult [N·m].
- `torsionEquivalentH({ H, T, Leff })` — Horizontal load equivalent to combined horizontal load and torsion on the effective area (DNV-RP-C212 5.4 / DNV Classification Notes 30.4): H′ = 2T/L′ + √(H² + (2T/L′)²). Returns `number`: H′ [N].
- `mudmatClassicalCapacity(o)` — Classical combined-loading check of a rectangular mudmat: Meyerhof effective area for the moments, equivalent horizontal load for torsion, Brinch Hansen or Vesic factors for inclination and shape (bearing.js), and a separate sliding check. Undrained: uniform su (use the strength at about B′/4 below skirt-tip level for a moderately increasing profile, or isoUndrainedCapacity in bearing.js with a Davis & Booker factor). Drained: c′–φ′. Returns `{Vult:number,ucBearing:number,Hres:number,Hequiv:number,Hult:number,ucSliding:number, Beff:number,Leff:number,Aeff:number,eB:number,eL:number,qult:number}`: forces [N], lengths [m], uc [-].
- `mudmatEnvelope(o)` — Failure-envelope check for a rectangular mat on undrained clay. Returns `{f:number,inside:boolean,v:number,hStar:number,mStar:number,loadFactor:number}`: f envelope value (≤ 1 inside) [-]; loadFactor: factor on (H, M, T) at constant V that reaches the envelope.
- `skirtPenetrationClay({ z, wallLength, t, su0, k = 0, alpha = 1, Nc = 7.5, gamma = 0 })` — Penetration resistance of mudmat skirts in clay with su = su0 + k z. Returns `{R:number,friction:number,tip:number}`: [N].
- `skirtPenetrationCpt({ z, qc, wallLength, t, kp, kf, n = 200 })` — CPT-based skirt penetration resistance (DNV-RP-C212 6.2 / DNV CN 30.4 4.5). Returns `{R:number,friction:number,tip:number}`: [N].
- `selfWeightPenetration(o)` — Self-weight penetration of skirts in clay: depth at which skirtPenetrationClay equals the available submerged weight, limited to the skirt height. Returns `{z:number,full:boolean,Rfull:number,ballastRequired:number}`: z [m]; resistance at full penetration [N]; extra weight needed to reach full penetration [N].
- `requiredMatSize(o)` — Smallest mat (fixed aspect ratio L/B) for which the classical bearing and sliding utilisations are both ≤ targetUc. Solved by bracketing and root-finding on B. Returns `{B:number,L:number,ucBearing:number,ucSliding:number}`: [m, m, -, -].
- `contactPressure({ B, L, V, ex = 0, ey = 0, n = 160 })` — Contact-pressure distribution under a rigid rectangular mat on a tensionless Winkler bed, bi-axial eccentricity, with lift-off. The pressure is the positive part of a plane p = p0 + gx·x + gy·y (origin at the centre); the three plane parameters are found from vertical and moment equilibrium by Newton iteration with numerical integration over the base (midpoint rule, n × n cells). Full contact reduces to p = V/A (1 ± 6 ex/B ± 6 ey/L); uniaxial lift-off to pmax = 2V / (3 L (B/2 − ex)). Returns `{pMax:number,pMin:number,p0:number,gx:number,gy:number,contactFraction:number, corners:{pp:number,pm:number,mp:number,mm:number},fullContact:boolean}`: pressures [Pa] (pMin is the plane value at the least-loaded corner, negative = lift-off there); gradients [Pa/m]; contact area ratio [-]; corners at (±B/2, ±L/2).
- `landingImpact({ mass, addedMass = 0, W, v, k })` — Landing of a structure on a soil bed idealised as a linear spring (energy balance, cf. DNV-RP-N103 6.2). Returns `{Fmax:number,delta:number,daf:number}`: peak soil reaction [N]; penetration [m]; Fmax/W′ [-].
- `landingVelocityLimit({ mass, addedMass = 0, W, k, Qallow })` — Largest set-down velocity for which the landing reaction of landingImpact stays within the allowable bearing load Qallow (ultimate capacity divided by the chosen factor). Returns `number`: limiting velocity [m/s].
- `breakoutForce({ W, A, su, breakoutFactor, Nc = 1.2 * (2 + PI), skirtFriction = 0 })` — Retrieval (breakout) force of a mat from clay – simple documented model. Returns `{F:number,suction:number,suctionPressure:number}`: [N, N, Pa].
- `onBottomStability({ W, FH, FL = 0, arm = 0, M0 = 0, B, mu = 0, su = 0, A = 0, Hpassive = 0 })` — On-bottom stability of a structure under hydrodynamic drag, inertia and lift (rigid-body statics). Returns `{fsSliding:number,fsOverturning:number,fsUplift:number,slidingResistance:number,e:number}`: [-,-,-,N,m]; e eccentricity of the base reaction [m].

## settlement.js — Settlement and consolidation

Sources: Boussinesq J. (1885); Newmark N.M. (1935) Simplified computation of vertical pressures in elastic foundations, Univ. Illinois Eng. Exp. Stn Circ. 24. Schleicher F. (1926); Timoshenko S. & Goodier J.N., Theory of Elasticity; Poulos H.G. & Davis E.H. (1974) Elastic Solutions for Soil and Rock Mechanics; Steinbrenner W. (1934). Schmertmann J.H., Hartman J.P. & Brown P.R. (1978) Improved strain influence factor diagrams, JGED 104. Janbu N. (1963) Soil compressibility as determined by oedometer and triaxial tests, ECSMFE Wiesbaden. Terzaghi K. (1943); Barron R.A. (1948) Trans. ASCE 113; Hansbo S. (1981); Carrillo N. (1942). Biot M.A. (1941) J. Appl. Phys. 12; Verruijt A. (2013) Theory and Problems of Poroelasticity.

- `boussinesqRectCorner(B, L, z)` — Vertical stress influence factor below the corner of a uniformly loaded rectangle on an elastic half-space (Newmark 1935 integration of Boussinesq). Returns `number`: Δσz / q [-] (0.25 at z = 0).
- `stressBelowRect({ q, B, L, z, x = 0, y = 0 })` — Vertical stress increment at any point below (or beside) a uniformly loaded rectangle, by superposition of four corner solutions. Returns `number`: Δσz [Pa].
- `stressBelowCircleCentre(q, R, z)` — Vertical stress increment on the axis of a uniformly loaded circle (Boussinesq). Returns `number`: Δσz [Pa].
- `stressPointLoad(Q, r, z)` — Boussinesq point load: Δσz = 3 Q z³ / (2π (r² + z²)^(5/2)). Returns `number`: Δσz [Pa].
- `stress2to1({ q, B, L = Infinity, z })` — 2:1 (vertical : horizontal) load-spread approximation: Δσz = q B L / ((B + z)(L + z)). Returns `number`: Δσz [Pa].
- `influenceRectCorner(m)` — Surface settlement influence factor at the corner of a flexible uniformly loaded rectangle on an elastic half-space (Schleicher 1926; Timoshenko & Goodier): s = q B (1 − ν²) I / E. Returns `number`: I [-].
- `steinbrennerFactors(M, N)` — Steinbrenner (1934) factors for the corner of a flexible rectangle B × L on a layer of thickness H over a rigid base: s = q B (1 − ν²) [F1 + (1 − 2ν)/(1 − ν) F2] / E, with M = L/B, N = H/B. Returns `{F1:number,F2:number}`: [-].
- `elasticSettlement({ q, B, L, E, nu, shape = 'rect', point = 'rigid', H = Infinity })` — Immediate (elastic) settlement of a uniformly loaded footing on a homogeneous elastic half-space or finite layer: s = q B (1 − ν²) I / E. Returns `{s:number,I:number}`: settlement [m]; influence factor [-].
- `footingRotation({ M, B, L, E, nu, shape = 'rect', Itheta })` — Rotation of a rigid footing under moment on an elastic half-space. Returns `{theta:number,differential:number}`: rotation [rad]; edge-to-edge differential settlement [m].
- `schmertmannSettlement({ B, L, dp, sigmaV0, gamma, layers, timeYears = 0.1, nSub = 40 })` — Schmertmann, Hartman & Brown (1978) strain-influence settlement of footings on sand. Returns `{s:number,C1:number,C2:number,Izp:number,sumIzEs:number}`: s [m]; Σ(Iz/Es)Δz [m/Pa].
- `janbuStrain({ sigma0, sigma1, m, j, sigmaRef = 100e3 })` — Janbu (1963) tangent-modulus strain for a stress change σ′0 → σ′1 with M = m σr (σ′/σr)^(1−j). Returns `number`: vertical strain [-].
- `oedometerStrain({ sigma0, dSigma, sigmaP, Cc, Cr, e0 })` — One-dimensional primary consolidation strain of a clay element (e–log σ′ model). Returns `number`: vertical strain [-].
- `consolidationSettlement({ q, B, L, shape = 'rect', stressMethod = 'boussinesq', sigmaV0Base = 0, layers, nSub = 10 })` — Primary consolidation settlement under a rectangular or circular footing by layer summation. Each layer is split into sub-layers; the stress increment at mid-height comes from Boussinesq (rectangle centre by corner integration, circle axis) or the 2:1 method. Returns `{s:number,layers:{s:number,zMid:number}[],profile:{z:number,sigma0:number,dSigma:number,strain:number}[]}`: s total settlement [m].
- `secondaryCompression({ Calpha, e0, H, t1, t2 })` — Secondary compression: s = Cα/(1 + e0) · H · log10(t2/t1) (Cα = Δe per log cycle of time). Returns `number`: settlement [m].
- `consolidationU(Tv)` — Terzaghi average degree of consolidation for uniform initial excess pore pressure (exact series). Returns `number`: U [-] in [0, 1].
- `consolidationTv(U)` — Inverse of consolidationU: time factor for a given average degree of consolidation. Returns `number`: Tv [-].
- `excessPorePressureRatio(Z, Tv)` — Excess pore pressure ratio at normalised depth Z = z/Hdr (exact Terzaghi series). Returns `number`: u/u0 [-].
- `radialConsolidation({ ch, t, de, dw, s = 1, khOverKs = 1, qw = Infinity, kh, z = 0, l = 0 })` — Radial consolidation to a vertical drain. Returns `{Ur:number,F:number,Th:number}`: [-].
- `combinedConsolidation(Uv, Ur)` — Carrillo (1942) combination of vertical and radial drainage: U = 1 − (1 − Uv)(1 − Ur). Returns `number`: U [-].
- `timeSettlementCurve({ times, si = 0, sc, cv, Hdr, Calpha = 0, e0 = 1, H = 0, tp })` — Time–settlement curve: s(t) = si + U(Tv) sc + secondary compression after tp. Returns `{t:number[],s:number[],U:number[],tp:number}`: settlements [m].
- `consolidationFD({ layers, load, tEnd, nSteps = 400, nCells = 40, top = 'drained', bottom = 'undrained', gammaW = 9810, theta = 1 })` — Finite-volume solution of one-dimensional consolidation in a layered column under time-dependent load. Returns `{t:Float64Array,settlement:Float64Array,U:Float64Array,z:Float64Array,u:Float64Array,sFinal:number}`: settlement(t) [m]; U = settlement / final settlement under the current load [-]; z cell centres [m]; u excess pore pressure at tEnd [Pa]; sFinal = Σ mv q(tEnd) h [m].
- `biotColumn({ q, h, K, G, k, alpha = 1, M = Infinity, gammaW = 9810, t, z })` — Biot poroelastic column under a suddenly applied surface load (Terzaghi's problem with compressible constituents; Verruijt 2013 ch. 2). With confined drained modulus Kv = K + 4G/3, Biot coefficient α and Biot modulus M (1/M = n/Kf + (α − n)/Ks). Returns `{p0:number,c:number,Tv:number,p:number,U:number,settlement:number,settlementImmediate:number, settlementFinal:number}`: pressures [Pa]; c [m^2/s]; settlements [m].

## earthpressure.js — Earth pressure, walls, sheet piles

Sources: Rankine W.J.M. (1857); Coulomb C.A. (1776); Jaky J. (1944); Mayne P.W. & Kulhawy F.H. (1982) K0–OCR. Okabe S. (1926); Mononobe N. & Matsuo H. (1929); Seed H.B. & Whitman R.V. (1970). Westergaard H.M. (1933) Water pressures on dams during earthquakes, Trans. ASCE 98. Terzaghi K. (1954) Anchored bulkheads, Trans. ASCE 119; Rowe P.W. (1952) Proc. ICE 1. Meyerhof G.G. (1953); BS 6349-2; EAU 2012; USS Steel Sheet Piling Design Manual (1984).

- `rankine(phi, beta = 0)` — Rankine coefficients for a vertical smooth wall and backfill sloping at β (pressure acts parallel to the slope): Ka,p = cos β [cos β ∓ √(cos²β − cos²φ)] / [cos β ± √(cos²β − cos²φ)]. For β = 0: Ka = tan²(π/4 − φ/2), Kp = tan²(π/4 + φ/2). Returns `{Ka:number,Kp:number}`: [-].
- `coulombKa({ phi, delta = 0, beta = 0, theta = 0 })` — Coulomb active coefficient (resultant inclined at δ to the wall normal). Returns `number`: Ka [-].
- `coulombKp({ phi, delta = 0, beta = 0, theta = 0 })` — Coulomb passive coefficient (plane wedge; unconservative for δ > φ/3, where log-spiral values such as Caquot & Kerisel should be used instead). Returns `number`: Kp [-].
- `k0(phi, ocr = 1)` — At-rest coefficient: Jaky (1944) K0 = 1 − sin φ′ for normally consolidated soil, extended for over-consolidation by Mayne & Kulhawy (1982): K0 = (1 − sin φ′) OCR^(sin φ′). Returns `number`: K0 [-].
- `tensionCrackDepth({ c, gamma, Ka, q = 0 })` — Depth of the tension crack behind a wall in c–φ soil (Rankine active): zc = (2c/√Ka − q) / γ ≥ 0. Returns `number`: zc [m].
- `pressureDiagram({ layers, state = 'active', waterTableDepth = Infinity, surcharge = 0, gammaW = 9810, tensionCrack = true })` — Lateral pressure diagram on a vertical wall in layered soil with a water table and uniform surcharge. Returns `{points:{z:number,sigmaV:number,u:number,sigmaH:number}[],Peff:number,Pwater:number,P:number, zBar:number,armAboveBase:number,H:number}`: points: effective vertical and horizontal stress and pore pressure [Pa] at each break (two entries at a layer interface); Peff, Pwater, P = total thrust [N/m]; zBar depth of the total resultant [m]; armAboveBase [m]; H total height [m].
- `stripSurchargePressure({ q, a, b, z })` — Horizontal stress on a rigid wall from a strip surcharge of intensity q parallel to the wall (elastic solution doubled for the rigid, non-yielding wall; Terzaghi 1954). Returns `number`: σh [Pa].
- `lineSurchargePressure({ Q, x, z, H })` — Horizontal stress on a rigid wall of height H from a line load Q (N/m) at distance mH, at depth nH (Boussinesq doubled, with Terzaghi's (1954) empirical modification for loads close to the wall). Returns `number`: σh [Pa].
- `mononobeOkabe({ phi, delta = 0, beta = 0, theta = 0, kh, kv = 0 })` — Mononobe–Okabe seismic earth-pressure coefficients (cohesionless, dry backfill). Returns `{KAE:number,KPE:number,psi:number}`: [-,-,rad].
- `westergaard({ kh, h, z, gammaW = 9810 })` — Westergaard (1933) hydrodynamic water pressure on a rigid vertical face during an earthquake (incompressible approximation): p(z) = (7/8) kh γw √(h z), total force (7/12) kh γw h² acting 0.4 h above the base. Returns `{p:number,force:number,armAboveBase:number}`: [Pa, N/m, m].
- `gravityWallStability({ B, vertical, horizontal, U = 0, xU, deltaBase, cBase = 0, Pp = 0, qult, globalFs })` — Stability of a gravity quay wall / caisson per metre run, from statics about the toe. Returns `{fsSliding:number,fsOverturning:number,e:number,N:number,H:number,qMeyerhof:number,qMax:number, qMin:number,fsBearing:number,inMiddleThird:boolean,globalFs:number|undefined}`: forces [N/m], q [Pa].
- `bollardPull({ pull, angle = 0, spreadLength, height })` — Effect of a bollard (mooring line) pull on a quay wall, per metre run: the horizontal component is distributed over an effective wall length and applied at cope level; the vertical component relieves the wall weight. Returns `{H:number,Vup:number,M:number}`: horizontal [N/m], uplift [N/m], overturning moment about the base [N·m/m].
- `sheetPileWall(o)` — Sheet-pile wall in cohesionless soil by limit equilibrium (free-earth support), per metre run. Returns `{d0:number,d:number,length:number,anchorForce:number,Mmax:number,zMmax:number,Pa:number,Pp:number}`: d0 equilibrium embedment [m]; d design embedment [m]; total length [m]; anchor force [N/m] (0 for cantilever); Mmax maximum bending moment [N·m/m] at depth zMmax [m]; thrusts at d0 [N/m].
- `deadmanAnchor({ gamma, H, Ka, Kp, b, shapeFactor = 1, safetyFactor = 1 })` — Ultimate resistance of a deadman anchor (anchor wall or block) in cohesionless soil near the surface, per metre run for a continuous wall: Tult = ½ γ H² (Kp − Ka), valid when the wall height h is at least about half the depth H to its base (Terzaghi 1943; Teng 1962). For a short block of width b the three-dimensional end effect is applied as a multiplier `shapeFactor` on the net pressure (argument; Ovesen's or Teng's charts), giving Tult = shapeFactor · ½ γ H² (Kp − Ka) · b. The deadman must lie outside the active wedge of the wall for the full value to apply. Returns `{Tult:number,Tallow:number}`: [N/m] for a continuous wall, [N] for a block.

## liquefaction.js — Liquefaction

Sources: Seed H.B. & Idriss I.M. (1971) Simplified procedure for evaluating soil liquefaction potential. Liao S.S.C. & Whitman R.V. (1986) Overburden correction factors for SPT in sand; and rd. ## Limits and open points

- Davis & Booker (1973) correction factor F and the Meyerhof & Hanna punching coefficient Ks are chart values: arguments, not built in.
- `isoScv` (ISO 19901-4 shape coefficient table) and the additive ISO Kc expression, the DNV skirt coefficients kp/kf quoted in `skirtPenetrationCpt`, the torsion-equivalent load `torsionEquivalentH`, the Rudolph & Bos coefficients, the Sheppard–Melville live-bed branch, the Burcharth damping coefficient a0, and the API RP 2SK / DNV-OS-E301 factor tables are quoted from the standards and papers as remembered and must be checked against the edition in force.
- Terzaghi Nγ uses a published closed-form approximation (about 10 % from the tabulated values) unless Kpγ is supplied; Skempton Nc is a fit that reproduces the chart end points.
- The mudmat failure envelope is a generic interaction with exact strip limits (Green; effective width); its exponents are neutral defaults, not a published fit. Published polynomial envelopes (Gourvenec 2007; Feng et al. 2014) are not implemented.
- Rigid rectangular footing settlement uses 0.93 × the flexible average; the rectangular rocking factor Iθ is an argument with a textbook default.
- Post-liquefaction strains use the Idriss & Boulanger (2008) closed forms of the Ishihara & Yoshimine relation; the Zhang et al. (2002) CPT equations are not implemented.
- Scour protection: only the critical-shear (static) approach is implemented; the De Vos et al. S3D dynamic-stability formula is not. Global scour and scour at large structures are user-coefficient rules.
- Drag-anchor power-law coefficients are indicative; the anchor trajectory is a simplified Neubecker & Randolph kinematic model; anchor piles are treated as rigid.
- Slope stability: circular surfaces only (ordinary and Bishop simplified). Spencer and Morgenstern–Price non-circular methods are not implemented.
- Site response is total-stress equivalent-linear; no pore-pressure generation or true nonlinear time-domain analysis.
- Bucket moment capacity is a rigid-rotation screening method without base resistance.
